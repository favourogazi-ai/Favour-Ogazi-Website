Operations

Building treasury and cross-border payment operations

Running operations for a B2B fintech in cross-border payments, foreign exchange and treasury, across trading, product and compliance.

OrganisationMighty
RoleOperations Lead
TypeOperations
WhenDec 2025 – now

01

Context

Mighty provides cross-border payments, foreign exchange and treasury infrastructure for businesses. As the business grew, its operations needed to keep pace across trading, product and compliance.

02

The challenge

Manual trading processes, many moving parts across payment and liquidity corridors, and teams that needed one shared operating rhythm.

03

What I did

  • Moved manual trading processes onto Mighty's in-house treasury platform, working with the product team on adoption, client walkthroughs and onboarding.
  • Oversee trade desk execution: client transactions, liquidity sourcing, settlement follow-up and reconciliation.
  • Coordinate due diligence and onboarding with compliance and legal, taking clients from documentation to live transactions.
  • Drive quarterly priorities, weekly reporting and scorecards, with operating rhythms and accountability built on EOS.

04

Outcome

—Trading volume growth
—Active clients
—Faster settlement

Figures to confirm with Favour. Percentages only.

05

What I learned

Operations in fintech is trust made visible. Every settled transaction is a promise kept.
