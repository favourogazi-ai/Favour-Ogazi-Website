2023 – 2025 · Fintech

DNI / DNI Pay

Business management & operations

Context
A new security startup that pivoted into DNI Pay, a digital payments platform for Africa. FONO was its operational and strategic backbone from day one.
What we did
Team structure, OKRs and hiring; a 12-month market entry plan; launch event; pitch decks, HR and finance policies; and, at go-live, customer experience, KYC, finance tracking and five new partnerships.
Results
2,000+ active users within 21 days of launch · 95%+ KYC verified within 24 hours · 90% of issues resolved within 24 hours · FONO's longest engagement, 2+ years.

“Building a business from scratch and running it after launch is the clearest proof of strategy-led execution.”

2024 – 2025 · Sports & entertainment

Lagos Liga

Social media marketing

Context
One of Africa's largest private recreational football leagues. Real energy on the pitch, but little of it online.
What we did
Took over five platforms at once with live event coverage, spotlighted every stakeholder in the league, and repositioned it as a content business that stays relevant between matches.
Results
Within one month: +1,868% impressions · +7,929% profile visits · +2,098% new followers · engagement up 75% to 5.1% · trending on X organically.

“When people feel important, they amplify for free.”

2023 – 2024 · Tech events & community

TechUnwind Fest

Marketing & partnerships

Context
A lifestyle-meets-tech festival for Nigeria's tech community. FONO led marketing and partnerships across three editions.
What we did
Hybrid digital and on-the-ground campaigns, 18+ brand partnerships built as mutual activations, an educate–entertain–sell content framework, press and influencer collaborations.
Results
3,000+ attendees across 3 editions · 40% attendee-to-user conversion · 650,000+ impressions · 75,000+ social users engaged.

“That alignment between event marketing and business goals takes strategy from the first invite to the last follow-up.”

2024 · Government / public sector

Kaduna State Government

Influencer marketing & campaign management

Context
The state needed to share progress on healthcare and education infrastructure without it feeling like a political advert.
What we did
Ran the influencer campaign end to end: a strict brief (no hashtags, no campaign language), six credible voices across X and Instagram, and a full analytics report.
Results
65,000+ total reach · all posts live within 48 hours of briefing · 100% tone compliance.

“The most effective government communication doesn't look like government communication.”

Business writing & production

Context
PTDF needed a STEM video that made complex science simple and inspiring for young Nigerians, without losing accuracy.
What we did
Led the proposal and won the project, then managed scripting, crew, shoot-day supervision and stakeholder coordination through to delivery of the film.

“Federal projects demand precision: many stakeholders, institutional sensitivities and zero tolerance for misalignment.”
