FONO

FONO Consulting is an advisory firm focused on helping business owners run their businesses with clarity, structure and purpose.

We work with founders and leadership teams on the parts of a business that decide whether it can grow: how it runs, how it's priced, how it reaches customers, and how the team works together. We give honest advice, and when it helps, we stay to see it through.

How we think

Advice that holds up on Monday morning.

Every business is different, so we don't arrive with a template. We start by listening: to the founder, the team, the customers and the numbers.

Then we tell you what we see, plainly, and build a plan grounded in your data and your reality. We care less about a beautiful strategy deck and more about whether things are working better three months later.

Where we advise

Five areas founders bring to us most.

  • Strategy & operations

    How the business really runs: structure, processes, decision rights and the weekly rhythm that keeps a team honest.

  • Pricing & business models

    What you charge, why, and whether the numbers actually work as you grow.

  • Growth & go-to-market

    Who you're for, how they find you, and the campaigns and partnerships that bring them in.

  • AI & automation

    Where automation saves real time in your operations, and where it would just add noise.

  • Leadership & team design

    Who does what, how people are hired and developed, and how a founder steps back without things falling apart.

How we engage

Three ways to work with us.

Diagnostic

A focused look at where the business is stuck. You get an honest written assessment and a clear plan you can act on.

Advisory retainer

Ongoing counsel for founders and leadership teams: regular working sessions, a thinking partner for big decisions, and accountability.

Embedded delivery

When a plan needs carrying through, we join for a defined project and run it alongside your team until it holds.

How an engagement runs

Listen, diagnose, build, review.

  1. Listen

    We learn the business, the people and what's actually in the way.

  2. Diagnose

    We name the real problem, which is rarely the first one mentioned.

  3. Build

    We design the plan and the systems, and work with your team to put them in place.

  4. Review

    We measure what changed, and adjust until it holds.
