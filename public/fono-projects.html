2023 – 2025 · Fintech

DNI / DNI Pay

Business management & operations

The situation
A young security startup that pivoted into DNI Pay, a payments platform for Africa. FONO was its operating and strategy partner from the first month.
What we did
Team structure, OKRs and hiring; a 12-month market entry plan; the launch event; pitch decks and policies; and, at go-live, support, KYC, finance tracking and five partnerships.
Results
2,000+ active users within 21 days of launch · 95%+ of KYC checks done within 24 hours · 90% of issues fixed within 24 hours · our longest engagement, 2+ years.
“Building a business and then running it after launch is the clearest test of strategy.”

2024 – 2025 · Sports & entertainment

Lagos Liga

Growth & communications

The situation
One of Africa's largest recreational football leagues, with real energy on the pitch and very little online.
What we did
Five platforms and live coverage at once, a spotlight on every stakeholder in the league, and a new position as a content business between matches.
Results
In one month: +1,868% impressions · +7,929% profile visits · +2,098% followers · engagement up 75% to 5.1% · trending on X without paid promotion.
“When people feel important, they amplify for free.”

2023 – 2024 · Tech events & community

TechUnwind Fest

Growth & partnerships

The situation
A lifestyle-meets-tech festival for Nigeria's tech community, across three editions.
What we did
Digital and on-the-ground campaigns, 18+ partnerships set up as shared activations, an educate–entertain–sell content plan, press and creator collaborations.
Results
3,000+ attendees across three editions · 40% of attendees became users · 650,000+ impressions · 75,000+ people engaged on social.
“Event marketing only works when it's tied to a business goal.”

2024 · Government / SME development

National MSME Conference 2024

Strategic communications

The situation
One of Nigeria's most important gatherings for small business.
What we did
One communications plan across social, email and community voices; daily recaps and personal reminders; live coverage, press and media.
Results
8,000+ applications · 800 in person at full capacity · 600+ online · 70% email open rate, 3.2× the industry average · 16% click-through.
“A high open rate is proof of trust. The audience wanted to hear from us.”

2024 · Government / public sector

Kaduna State Government

Public communications

The situation
The state wanted to share real progress in healthcare and education without it sounding like a political advert.
What we did
A strict brief (no hashtags, no campaign language), six credible voices across X and Instagram, and a full report at the end.
Results
65,000+ people reached · every post live within 48 hours of the brief · 100% of posts on tone.
“The best government communication doesn't look like government communication.”

2024 · Federal government / STEM education

Petroleum Technology Development Fund (PTDF)

Project leadership & production

The situation
PTDF needed a STEM film that made complex science feel simple and exciting for young Nigerians, without losing accuracy.
What we did
Led the proposal that won the project, then managed the script, crew, shoot and every stakeholder through to delivery.
Results
Delivered “Empowering the Future: PTDF STEM in Action” on a tight timeline, with warm feedback from every stakeholder.
“Federal projects need precision: many stakeholders, real sensitivities, no room for misalignment.”

2025 · Civic advocacy

TOS Group: Reserved Seats Bill for Women

Public affairs & campaigns

The situation
A campaign to get people talking about guaranteed seats for women in Nigeria's legislature.
What we did
Ten days across four platforms: daily content, live engagement, 10+ creators, two new pages built from scratch, and content in English, Yoruba and Igbo.
Results
145,000+ views in 10 days · 12,200 TikTok views on a brand-new page · 10+ creators · 3 languages.
“Treat every day of a campaign as its own small launch.”
