About

One builder

I'm Favour, an operations leader who turns strategy into systems that work. I bring clarity to complex problems and help teams execute with ownership.

Today I lead operations at Mighty, a B2B fintech in cross-border payments and treasury. Before that, I helped build DNI Pay from a young startup into a live payments platform. Through FONO, my advisory firm, I've worked with 18 businesses and institutions on pricing, operations, growth and AI.

I'm also a writer and an avid reader. Writing is how I process what I see, think and feel, and thousands of readers follow my ideas on Substack, Medium and X. I believe documentation and clear communication are leadership skills, and I practise both.

Outside work, I'm a woman of faith who cares deeply about her people. I serve in Children's Church, treasure my friendships, and love good stories, travel, movies and finding humour in ordinary things. I want a full life, with room for ambition, love, curiosity and quiet.
