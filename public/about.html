About

Hello, I'm Favour.

I never planned to work in operations. A mix of chance, curiosity and drive took me there.

I studied microbiology. I sold beauty products in school, helped run a student union election campaign, started FONO, and went back for a master's in Business and Management. Somewhere along the way I realised the thing I'm best at is the part most people find boring: turning a good idea into a way of working that holds up.

Most businesses don't stall for lack of ideas. They stall because nobody has built the structure, the routines and the trust that let an idea run without the founder holding it together. That's my part. Today I do it at Mighty, a cross-border payments company, and for the founders FONO works with. Before that, I helped build DNI Pay from a young startup into a live payments platform.

I'm also a writer and a big reader. Writing is how I process what I see, think and feel, and thousands of people read my ideas on Substack, Medium and X.

Outside work, I'm a woman of faith and I serve in Children's Church. I treasure my friends, love a good story, travel when I can, watch too many movies, and find the funny side of ordinary days. I want a full life, with room for ambition, love, curiosity and quiet.

How I work

Three habits.

I write things down.

Work you can't explain is work that can't be repeated. I document what was done, why, and what happened, so the next person doesn't start from zero.

I talk early and often.

Most problems I've fixed were communication problems first. Plans only work when the people carrying them understand them.

I let tools do the boring parts.

Automation and AI should give people their time back for the work that needs judgement.
