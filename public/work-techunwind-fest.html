Projects

Filling TechUnwind Fest, three times

Marketing and partnerships for three editions of a lifestyle-meets-tech festival, with partnerships built as real activations.

OrganisationTechUnwind Fest · through FONO
My roleMarketing & partnerships
TypeProjects
When2023 – 2024

What was going on

TechUnwind Fest gives Nigeria's tech community, from founders and creatives to investors, a relaxed place to meet and learn. FONO led marketing and partnerships across three editions.

The challenge

Get the right people in the room, and make the event worth it for the brands supporting it.

What I did

  • Ran digital campaigns alongside volunteers who reached tech communities in person.
  • Brought in and managed 18+ brand partnerships, each set up as a shared activation rather than a logo on a banner.
  • Built the content around three ideas: educate, entertain, sell. Spaces, lives, radio, street interviews and a talent hunt.
  • Chose influencers for their record of converting people, not their follower counts.

What changed

40%Attendees who became users
3,000+Attendees across 3 editions
18+Brand partnerships

45% of the audience were mid-level professionals and 25% senior experts.

What I took from it

Event marketing works when it serves a business goal, from the first invite to the last follow-up.
