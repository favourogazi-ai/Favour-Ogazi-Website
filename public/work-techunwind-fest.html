Marketing and partnerships for TechUnwind Fest

Three editions of a lifestyle-meets-tech festival, filled through hybrid marketing and partnerships built as real activations.

OrganisationTechUnwind Fest · via FONO
RoleMarketing & partnerships lead
TypeProjects
When2023 – 2024
  • Context
  • The challenge
  • What I did
  • Outcome
  • What I learned

01

Context

TechUnwind Fest gives Nigeria's tech community, from founders and creatives to investors and professionals, a relaxed space to connect and learn. FONO led marketing and partnership strategy across three editions.

02

The challenge

Get the right people in the room, and turn attendance into real interest for the brands behind the event.

03

What I did

  • Designed a hybrid strategy: targeted digital campaigns plus volunteers reaching tech communities on the ground.
  • Secured and managed 18+ brand partnerships, each structured as a mutual activation rather than a logo placement.
  • Built content around three principles: educate, entertain, sell. Spaces, lives, radio, interviews and a talent hunt.
  • Chose influencers for proven conversion, not follower counts, and secured press features.

04

Outcome

40%Attendee-to-user conversion
3,000+Attendees across 3 editions
18+Brand partnerships

Audience: 45% mid-level professionals, 25% senior experts.

05

What I learned

Event marketing works when it serves a business goal, from the first invite to the last follow-up.
