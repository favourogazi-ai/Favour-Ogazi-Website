Operations

Building treasury and cross-border payment operations

Running the operating side of a B2B fintech that moves money across borders, from the trade desk to the weekly scorecard.

OrganisationMighty
My roleOperations Lead
TypeOperations
WhenDec 2025 – now

What was going on

Mighty gives businesses cross-border payments, foreign exchange and treasury infrastructure. When I joined, the company was growing faster than its processes. A lot of trading still happened by hand, and trading, product and compliance each had their own way of working.

The challenge

Money only moves well when everything around it is in step: liquidity in the right place, a trade desk that executes cleanly, onboarding that doesn't stall, and teams that agree on what matters this week.

What I did

  • Moved manual trading onto Ramp, Mighty's in-house treasury product, working with the product team on adoption, client walkthroughs and onboarding.
  • Run trade desk execution day to day: client transactions, liquidity sourcing, settlement follow-up and reconciliation with traders and partners.
  • Work with compliance and legal to close documentation gaps and get new clients to their first live transaction.
  • Set quarterly priorities, weekly reporting and scorecards for trading volume, revenue and active clients, using EOS to build the operating rhythm.

What changed

—Trading volume growth
—Active clients
—Faster settlement

Outcomes coming soon.

What I took from it

In payments, operations is how trust becomes visible. Every settled transaction is a promise kept.
