Communications for the National MSME Conference

Communications for one of Nigeria's biggest small-business gatherings, from the first invitation to the follow-up after.

OrganisationNational MSME Conference · through FONO
My roleStrategic communications
TypeProjects
When2024

What was going on

The National MSME Conference is one of Nigeria's most important gatherings for small and medium businesses. FONO handled the communications, from early awareness to live coverage and follow-up.

The challenge

Fill a full-capacity venue, get people to show up online, and keep every message personal at national scale.

What I did

  • Ran one plan across LinkedIn, X, Instagram, email and business community voices.
  • Made email the engine: daily recaps and personal reminders that brought people back.
  • Handled live coverage, press, interviews and media releases with one consistent voice.

What changed

70%Email open rate, 3.2× industry
16%Click-through rate
8,000+Applications received

800 people attended in person, at full capacity, and 600+ joined online.

What I took from it

A 70% open rate is really a measure of trust. People open what they've learned to expect something useful from.
