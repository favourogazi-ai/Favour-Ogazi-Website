Strategic communications for the National MSME Conference

Integrated communications for one of Nigeria's biggest small-business gatherings, from first invite to post-event follow-up.

01

Context

The National MSME Conference is one of Nigeria's most significant gatherings for small and medium enterprise development. FONO handled strategic communications from pre-event awareness to live coverage and follow-up.

02

The challenge

Fill a full-capacity event, drive online attendance, and keep every message personal at national scale.

03

What I did

  • Ran an integrated strategy across LinkedIn, X, Instagram, email and influencer coordination with business leaders.
  • Made email the engine: daily recaps and personalised reminder sequences to drive virtual attendance.
  • Managed live coverage, press, interviews and media releases with consistent messaging throughout.

04

Outcome

70%Email open rate, 3.2× industry
16%Click-through rate
8,000+Applications received

800 in-person attendees (full capacity) and 600+ online.

05

What I learned

A 70% open rate is a measure of trust. People open what they've learned to expect value from.
