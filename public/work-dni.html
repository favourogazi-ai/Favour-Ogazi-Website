Operations

Building DNI Pay's operations from the ground up

From a new security startup to a live payments platform: team structure, strategy, launch and the systems that run it.

OrganisationDNI / DNI Pay
RoleHead of Business Management & Operations
TypeOperations
When2023 – 2025

01

Context

DNI began in August 2023 as a new tech startup focused on estate and building security. By the end of 2024 it had pivoted and relaunched as DNI Pay, a digital payments platform built to make transactions faster, safer and more accessible across Africa.

02

The challenge

A young company needed an operational and strategic backbone: structure, hiring, a go-to-market plan, and systems ready for a live payments product.

03

What I did

  • Redefined the team structure, set organisational OKRs and built the first hiring processes for design, development and sales.
  • Wrote a 12-month market entry plan, overhauled every digital platform and led the company's first major public launch event.
  • Updated pitch decks and company profiles for funding conversations, introduced HR and finance policies, negotiated vendor contracts and managed budgets.
  • When DNI Pay went live in August 2025, built customer experience, KYC verification, HR and finance tracking systems, plus five new partnership agreements.

04

Outcome

95%+KYC verified within 24 hours
90%Issues resolved within 24 hours
<10 minAverage response time

Also: 2,000+ active users within 21 days of launch, 5 new partnerships and 2 new markets scoped.

05

What I learned

Building a business from scratch, then running it after go-live, is a different discipline from advising one. Strategy only counts when it ships.
