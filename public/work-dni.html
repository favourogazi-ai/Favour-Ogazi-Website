Operations

Building DNI Pay's operations from the ground up

From a young security startup to a live payments platform: structure, hiring, strategy, launch, and everything needed to run it afterwards.

OrganisationDNI / DNI Pay
My roleBusiness management & operations
TypeOperations
When2023 – 2025

What was going on

DNI started in August 2023 as a tech startup focused on estate and building security. By the end of 2024 it had pivoted and relaunched as DNI Pay, a payments platform built to make transactions faster, safer and easier across Africa. I was there from the start.

The challenge

A young company with a big idea and very little structure. It needed a team, a plan for the market, policies people could follow, and systems ready for real money on day one.

What I did

  • Designed the team structure, set company OKRs and built the first hiring processes for design, development and sales.
  • Wrote a 12-month market entry plan, rebuilt the digital platforms and led the company's first big public launch.
  • Updated the pitch decks for funding conversations, introduced HR and finance policies, negotiated vendor contracts and managed budgets.
  • When DNI Pay went live in August 2025, set up customer support, KYC verification, HR records and finance tracking, and signed five new partnerships.

What changed

95%+KYC checks done within 24 hours
90%Transaction issues fixed within 24 hours
<10 minAverage response to users

Also: 2,000+ active users within 21 days of launch, five new partnerships and two new markets researched.

What I took from it

Advising a business and running one are different jobs. This was the one where the strategy had to work on Monday morning.
